// Our Story

A Kitchen Born from Passion

Foodiecious began in 2012 as a tiny family kitchen on Flavor Avenue with just five tables and one unwavering belief: food made with love tastes better. Our founder, Maria Delgado, grew up watching her grandmother cook slow-simmered sauces and hand-rolled pastas, and wanted to share that warmth with the world.

What started as a weekend pop-up quickly became the talk of Downtown City. Within two years we had tripled our kitchen, trained a team of 25 chefs, and earned our first culinary recognition. Today, Foodiecious is a destination — but we've never lost sight of what brought us here: honest, soulful, unforgettable food.
